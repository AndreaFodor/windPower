SUMMARY (as of now):

- Excluding features other than wind speed and temperature seem to have made the model better.
- Cubing speed seem to improve the model
- Cubing speed and dividing by temperature seem to have a very slight impact
- Including radius squared have not seem to improve the model (probably because most wind turbines are roughly similar size)



Best results for different approaches:

1. Only the feature wind speed:
    Best combination in terms of mean MAPE: pca = 36, nbr = 5, MAPE = 0.181 ± 0.029, R2 = 0.911 ± 0.015 
    Best combination in terms of mean R2 score: pca = 36, nbr = 5, MAPE = 0.181 ± 0.029, R2 = 0.911 ± 0.015
